# ISCC v2 — surface brief: index.html (Visual Concept case study)

Mode: Experience. Code-led. One file, offline, no build step.

## Direction contract

THESIS. The championship is shown as a broadcast, not a style guide: light, smoke, metal and a live HUD carry the argument. Refused: the centered-H2-plus-equal-grid specimen page, and any "N things, one thing" headline in either language.

OWN-WORLD. Five hues, 70/20/10, two arenas. The two brand hues are the two sides: CT is Legacy Blue, T is Cup Gold. Smoke is the glass. Grenade arcs and the spray climb are the ornament. Square plates, 14px on glass only, no shadows, light behind ink, grain under everything.

STORY. In three seconds: this is Counter-Strike, and it is a school championship. Then: every mark, token and overlay can be lifted and used.

FIRST VIEWPORT. Full-bleed WebGL rig: blue shafts from the left, gold from the right, converging on a rendered cup low-centre; 百炼成徽 in CN Display across the lower third; a cropped "26" bleeding off the right edge; scorebug-style nav strip on top.

FORM. Scroll case study in ten plates (cover, mark, light, smoke, metal, broadcast, color, type, posters, footer), each a single screenshot. Signature interaction: the punchable smoke. Motion grammar: beam-plus-smoke stinger between plates, arcs drawn on scroll, scorebug running a scripted round. seed: none (pinned) — "Rebuild v1 into a Visual Concept case study in the BUCK register".

FINISH. unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## What changes / what stays

Stays: palette and ratio, both arenas and both tongues with persistence, the four slogans, the four typefaces as embedded subsets, pure-path SVG exports and the sheet, no shadows, square plates, reduced motion, concept-only footer.

Changes: the logo (three directions, user picks), one emblem pasted fifteen times becomes one hero mark with a derived family, CSS-diagram materials become rendered ones (shader beams, refracting reeded glass, a lit cup), template rhythm becomes asymmetric plates, fade-and-rise becomes authored sequences, and a CS2 vocabulary enters throughout.
