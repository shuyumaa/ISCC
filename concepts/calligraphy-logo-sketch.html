Each mark is a written character, not a drawn icon. The brush carries the school: discipline, practice, precision. One stroke carries Counter-Strike. The seal, the way a calligrapher signs a scroll, carries ISCC and becomes the favicon, where brushwork cannot survive.

1武止戈为武

Stopping the weapon is true skill

  • The dot of 戈 is the round: one gold mark in a wet ink stroke.
  • CS: the slash of 戈 is the tracer; the CT side literally stops the weapon.
  • School value: discipline and restraint. A competition about control, not violence.

2永永字八法

The eight principles of 永

  • Every calligraphy student's first character: it holds all eight basic strokes.
  • CS: the first stroke, the dot, is the first shot. Fundamentals before flair.
  • School value: practice. 百炼 is the eight strokes, a thousand times.

3中命中

Hit the mark

  • Read zhòng, 中 means to hit the target. The long hanging-needle stroke pierces the box.
  • CS: where the needle crosses the box it opens into a crosshair gap, with the round in the centre.
  • School value: precision and focus, the shooter's and the scholar's.
